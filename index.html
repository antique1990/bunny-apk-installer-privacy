Bunny Apk installer — Privacy Policy

Publisher: AntiqueDev · Effective date: 3 November 2026

简体中文

Local operation

Bunny Apk installer reads the Android packages you choose on your Windows PC and uses the Android Debug Bridge (ADB) tools to send them to the Android device you select. The application does not provide an app account system, advertising, analytics, or a service that uploads your packages to the developer's cloud.

Information stored on your PC

The application stores your language, appearance and configured ADB location locally. It keeps up to 100 installation results, including time, file name, package name and result. Device identifiers for connections you choose to hide are stored locally so that the choice persists. Package archives can be extracted into a local temporary folder; temporary copies are normally removed when the operation ends.

You can clear the installation history in the app and restore hidden connections. Settings and local records are normally stored in the current user's application data. A packaged installation may have Windows-managed app data redirection.

Android and local-network connections

USB debugging and wireless debugging require your permission on the Android device. Local-network service discovery and ADB connections exchange network and device information with your selected device and local network. Temporary QR pairing credentials are held in memory and removed when the session ends; Bunny does not write pairing codes to its history or command-line arguments. ADB and Android manage their own debugging keys and pairing authorizations. Remove trusted computers or revoke debugging authorizations in Android settings when appropriate.

Optional exports and support

When you choose to export results, Bunny writes the selected history information to the local file you select. If you later share that file or contact support, the information you voluntarily send is handled by the chosen communication service. Avoid sending pairing codes or private package files in a support request.

Microsoft Store and external tools

Microsoft handles Store purchases, licensing and distribution under its own privacy terms. Official Android SDK Platform Tools are installed separately and have their own terms. Bunny's local processing statements do not describe data independently collected by your operating system, Microsoft Store, Android or external support services.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, email bunnybtc100@gmail.com, use the support contact shown on the Bunny Apk installer Microsoft Store listing, or visit the publisher privacy-support page. This page will be updated if the application's data handling changes.

Bunny Apk installer 隐私政策

发布者：AntiqueDev。生效日期：2026 年 11 月 3 日。

本地处理

Bunny 在 Windows 电脑上读取你选择的 Android 安装包，并通过 ADB 工具发送到你指定的 Android 设备。应用不提供自有账号系统、广告、遥测分析或向开发者云端上传安装包的服务。

本机保存的信息

应用在本机保存语言、外观和 ADB 路径；最近最多 100 条安装结果，包括时间、文件名、包名和结果；以及你选择隐藏的设备标识。容器包可能临时解压到本机，通常在操作结束后移除。可在应用中清空历史或恢复隐藏连接。打包安装时，本地数据可能由 Windows 重定向至应用专用位置。

设备和局域网

USB 和无线调试需要在 Android 设备上授权。局域网服务发现及 ADB 连接会与所选设备和本地网络交换网络、设备信息。二维码临时配对凭据保留于内存，结束后移除；Bunny 不将配对码写入历史或命令行。ADB 和 Android 自行管理调试密钥及配对授权，可在 Android 设置中移除可信电脑或撤销授权。

导出、支持和第三方

主动导出时，结果写入你选择的本地文件。若之后分享文件或联系支持，你主动发送的信息由所用通信服务处理；请勿发送配对码或私密安装包。商店购买、授权和分发由微软按其隐私条款处理；官方 Android SDK Platform Tools 需另行安装并受其条款约束。本政策的本地处理说明不涵盖操作系统、微软商店、Android 或支持平台独立处理的数据。

联系与更新

隐私问题请发送邮件至 bunnybtc100@gmail.com，也可使用 Bunny Apk installer 微软商店页面提供的支持联系方式，或发布者的隐私支持页面。应用的数据处理方式变化时，本页将相应更新。
